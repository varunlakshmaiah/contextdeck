# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Welcome people of every background and experience level.
- Give and take criticism about the work, not the person.
- Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated anywhere in this project's spaces.

To report unacceptable behaviour privately, use [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/varunlakshmaiah/contextdeck/security/advisories/new); it's the one private channel to the maintainer, [@varunlakshmaiah](https://github.com/varunlakshmaiah), on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block anyone who doesn't follow this code.
